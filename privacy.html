Privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026.

ReliantOS rclone is a private tool used only by its owner. It signs in to the owner's own Google account with his permission and copies files from his own computer to his own Google Drive.

What it accesses

Google Drive files and folders in the owner's account, using the full Google Drive access he grants at sign-in. They are read only to copy his files there and to check what is already there.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect, sell or share any data with anyone.
  • It does not send Google user data anywhere except between the owner's computer and his own Google Drive.
  • It has no other users and no advertising or analytics.

Storage

The sign-in token is kept only on the owner's computer and in its own encrypted backups.

Retention and deletion

No copy of Google user data is kept outside the owner's computer, his backups and his own Google Drive. To remove access, revoke it at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the rclone configuration file on the owner's computer.

Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at this site's GitHub repository.
